How the work is made

Built for reuse.
Released with evidence.

These are not one-off prompt collections. A public Skill needs a trigger boundary, useful output contract, validation evidence, and a clear statement of what it will not do.

  1. Make the job explicit.One repeatable outcome, with a clear input and output boundary.
  2. Test real triggers.Confirm that the Skill activates for the right request and stays out of unrelated work.
  3. Publish responsibly.Keep secrets, personal material, upstream work, and unsupported claims out of the release.

The boundary matters

A portfolio is also a record of restraint.

Every locally installed Skill is classified before it appears as personal work. Original projects are published. Upstream packages retain attribution. Machine-specific, private, permission-bound, or unsafe material stays out of public release.
