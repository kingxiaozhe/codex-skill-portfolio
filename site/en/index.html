Designed for reuse. Validated for delivery.

These are not one-off prompt collections. A public Skill needs a clear trigger boundary, an output contract, validation evidence, and a statement of what it will not do.

  1. Define the job.One repeatable outcome with a clear input and output boundary.
  2. Test real triggers.Confirm that the Skill starts for the right request and stays out of unrelated work.
  3. Publish responsibly.Keep secrets, personal material, upstream work, and unsupported claims out of the release.

A portfolio should record restraint, too.

Every locally installed Skill is classified before it appears as personal work. Original projects are published. Upstream packages keep attribution. Machine-specific, private, permission-bound, or unsafe material stays out of public release.
